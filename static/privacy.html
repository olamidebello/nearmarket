Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Operator and scope

Olamide Olatayo Bello Corporation operates NearMarket. This notice describes information handled by the platform. Hosting providers, payment providers, and independently administered marketplaces may also process information under their own policies.

Information collected

Accounts include your name, email address, a password hash, role, membership, and account status. We store listings, uploaded photos, messages, favorites, reports, order records, pickup confirmations, and disputes. Security and administration records include sessions, API key hashes and usage, audit events, and payment references. The host or reverse proxy may also keep request metadata such as IP addresses, timestamps, and errors.

Location and address information

With your browser permission, the site requests GPS coordinates to find eligible local listings and check trading radius. The default is 60 miles; you can change your radius within marketplace limits. Saved buy/sell preferences, listing centers, and address labels can contain precise locations and are stored on the server when you save them. Current-location searches and checkout send coordinates to the server; current GPS is refreshed when needed rather than continuously tracked in the background. Deny or revoke GPS permission in your browser and choose a fixed location if permitted. Geographic features require a usable location.

Public and restricted information

Listings expose seller display names, item details, uploaded images, public area descriptions, approximate coordinates, and distance/radius information. Exact saved listing centers and private address labels are restricted in the application, but authorized super administrators can access them for administration. Messages and orders are available to the relevant participants; authorized operators can access stored records for support, safety, and administration. Tenant administrators can access records their role permits. Avoid putting personal information in public descriptions or photos, including embedded image metadata.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts, marketplace membership, local discovery, messages, checkout, pickup, moderation, fraud prevention, API access controls, troubleshooting, and legal compliance. Service operators and administrators receive information needed for their duties. Records may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address fraud, safety, or legal claims.

Payment and address providers

Stripe or Paystack receives checkout information, such as order amounts, references, and the buyer email needed by the selected integration. Card details are entered on the payment provider’s hosted pages; NearMarket’s checkout integration does not store full card details. If address search is configured and you press Search, your search text is sent through the server to Geoapify. Address results may be cached for your account and marketplace for seven days. Do not submit an address query you do not want that provider to process. Review each provider’s privacy policy before using its service.

Cookies and device storage

The site uses a session cookie to keep you signed in, with a seven-day lifetime. Signing out ends that session. Browser storage may retain interface preferences such as marketplace selection. The current application does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. Browser controls can clear cookies and local storage; doing so may sign you out or reset preferences.

Retention, protection, and choices

Application records and backups are retained until removed through an operational or legal process; most records do not currently have automatic deletion schedules. Passwords and API access keys are stored as hashes; managed payment secrets are encrypted. These controls do not eliminate every security risk. You can edit your listings and location preferences, revoke browser GPS permission, and sign out. Account export and deletion are not currently self-service: request assistance from the operator. Requests may require identity verification, and transaction, security, or legal records may need to be retained. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location.

Children, processing locations, and updates

The service is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Notify the operator if you believe a child has supplied account information. Hosting and third-party providers may process information in countries different from yours; processing locations depend on the deployment and provider configuration. Changes to this notice will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For account, privacy, or copyright requests, obtain the operator’s verified contact details from your marketplace administrator. Do not post passwords, payment credentials, identity documents, or private addresses in public listings. A dedicated public legal contact has not yet been published.
